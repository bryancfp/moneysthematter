The story

Why does money work at all?

Before there were coins, there was memory. Before there were banks, there was trust.

Money is so deeply woven into everyday life that it can feel almost natural. We earn it, save it, spend it, invest it, fear losing it, and rarely stop to ask why any of it works.

This episode begins with the ordinary difficulty of exchange: finding someone who wants what we have and who also has what we need. From there, it follows the story toward memory, obligation, trust, trade, and the shared belief that gives money its power.

Money works because enough people agree that it does.
