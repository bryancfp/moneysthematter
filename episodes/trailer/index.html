About the show

Money is never just about money.

Behind every market, currency, bank, and financial system is a human story.

Money's the Matter explores the history, psychology, and ideas behind the financial world through stories about trust, ambition, fear, innovation, and the strange agreements that connect us.

Season One begins at the beginning: with the ordinary problem of exchanging value, the rise of cities, and the systems people created when cooperation grew too large for memory alone.

Money tells the story of civilization.

Trailer transcript

Welcome to Money's the Matter.

This transcript has been lightly edited for clarity and readability.

There are objects we pass every day without giving them a second thought.

A worn dollar bill folded gently into a wallet.

A credit card tapped against a reader, anxiously awaiting that familiar beep.

A stock certificate, unsigned, collecting dust.

A number glowing quietly on a phone screen—a green number fueling joy, while a red number crushes the spirit.

They're so familiar that it's easy to forget how strange they really are, because none of them has value on its own.

Their value lives somewhere else.

In trust. In belief. In stories we've been telling ourselves for thousands of years.

Money is an illusion nestled in our imagination.

But that doesn't mean nature has no system of accounting.

I'm Bryan. I've spent the last fifteen years working in finance, helping families make some of the biggest financial decisions of their lives.

But the questions that have stayed with me were never really just about investing.

They were questions about people.

Why do bubbles happen, over and over?

Why does fear spread faster than confidence?

How did a handful of merchants in Amsterdam change the way the entire world invests?

Why does a piece of paper printed by a government become something we trade our time, our labor, or even our lives for?

Those questions eventually led me somewhere unexpected: history, psychology, economics, politics, and philosophy.

Because the deeper I looked, the harder it became to separate money from the people who created it.

This podcast is an exploration of those stories.

Some episodes will take us to crowded trading floors and forgotten banks. Others will begin in kitchens, factories, harbors, or around family dinner tables.

Every one of those stories starts with people, because money has always been a human story.

So, if you're curious about how we got here, if you've ever wondered why markets behave the way they do, or if you've simply found yourself asking questions that don't fit neatly into a textbook, I hope you'll join me.

This is Money's the Matter.
