About the podcast

Money is never just money.

Money's the Matter is a narrative podcast about financial history, economics, markets, and the psychology of money. Each episode starts with a story and follows the people, incentives, institutions, and ideas that shaped the financial world we live in today.

The show

History explains more than a chart can.

Why do people trust money? Why do bubbles keep happening? Why does inflation feel different from the statistics? Why did the United States create a central bank? Those questions make more sense when you know the stories that came before them.

The show explores subjects such as the origins of money, coins and paper currency, market bubbles, banking crises, inflation, financial psychology, and the evolution of modern markets. The goal is not to turn history into trivia. It is to use history to make the present easier to understand.

The host

Bryan Yach, CFP®

Bryan Yach is a financial planner and the host of Money's the Matter. He has more than fifteen years of experience helping families navigate investing, retirement, and major financial decisions.

The podcast grew from a simple idea: financial concepts become easier to understand when they are connected to the people, choices, mistakes, and incentives that created them.

Episodes are created for education and general information. They are not individualized investment, tax, legal, or financial advice.

Editorial approach

Stories first. Sources matter.

Money's the Matter aims to make complicated financial and economic ideas understandable without flattening the history behind them. Episode pages can include transcripts and source notes so listeners can explore the underlying material and distinguish historical fact from interpretation.
